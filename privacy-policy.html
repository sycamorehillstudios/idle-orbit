Idle Orbit - Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 2, 2026

Overview

Idle Orbit ("the App") is a free-to-play idle game. This privacy policy explains what data we collect and how it is used.

Data We Collect

Game Progress: Your game state (resources, satellites, upgrades, prestige data) is saved locally on your device. If you opt into Google Play Games cloud save, your game state is also stored in your Google account.

Google Play Games Services: If you sign in to Google Play Games, we access your public gaming profile (display name) for leaderboard functionality. We do not collect or store your Google account email or personal information.

Advertising: The App uses Google AdMob to display optional rewarded video ads. AdMob may collect device identifiers and usage data for ad personalization in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy. You can opt out of personalized ads in your device settings.

Analytics: The App does not use any analytics SDKs. We do not track your usage patterns, location, or browsing activity.

Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or otherwise transfer your personal information to third parties. The only third-party services used are Google Play Games Services (for leaderboards and cloud save) and Google AdMob (for rewarded ads), both governed by Google's privacy policy.

Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe we have inadvertently collected such information, please contact us so we can delete it.

Data Retention

Game save data is stored locally on your device and can be deleted by uninstalling the App or using the in-app reset function. Cloud save data is stored in your Google account and can be managed through Google Play Games settings.

Your Rights

You can delete your local game data at any time using the "Reset Game" option in Settings. You can revoke Google Play Games access through your Google account settings. You can opt out of personalized ads in your device's ad settings.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date above.
