Idle Orbit - Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 30, 2026

Overview

Idle Orbit ("the App") is a free-to-play idle game for Android and for iPhone and iPad (iOS), published by Sycamore Hill Studios ("we", "us"). This privacy policy explains what data the App handles on each platform and how it is used.

In short: the App has no user accounts, we do not operate any server that receives your data, and we do not sell or share your personal information. The only services the App talks to are the ones built into your platform: Google's on Android and Apple's on iOS.

Data We Collect

Game Progress: Your game state (resources, satellites, upgrades, prestige data, settings and purchases) is saved locally on your device. We never receive it. If you use cloud save, a copy is also stored by your platform provider, not by us: on Android, if you opt into Google Play Games cloud save, your game state is also stored in your Google account; on iOS, if you use Game Center saved games, your game state is stored by Apple in your iCloud account.

Google Play Games Services (Android): If you sign in to Google Play Games, we access your public gaming profile (display name) for leaderboard functionality. We do not collect or store your Google account email or personal information. Your scores and achievements are sent to Google Play Games so they can appear on its leaderboards and achievement lists.

Apple Game Center (iOS): On iOS, the App uses Apple Game Center for sign-in, leaderboards, achievements and saved games. If you are signed in to Game Center, the App receives your Game Center nickname to show it in the App, and sends your scores and achievements to Game Center. Your nickname and scores may be visible to other players on Game Center leaderboards, according to your Game Center settings. Saved games are stored by Apple in your iCloud account (this requires iCloud Drive to be turned on). We do not receive your Apple Account email, name or any other personal information. Game Center and iCloud are provided by Apple and governed by Apple's Privacy Policy.

Purchases: In-app purchases, including the Idle Orbit Premium subscription, are processed by Google Play on Android and by the Apple App Store on iOS. We never receive your payment details. The App receives a confirmation from the store so it can deliver what you bought, and records your purchases in your game save on your device. Purchase records are kept by Google or Apple under their own privacy policies.

Notifications: If you allow notifications, the App schedules reminders (for example, when your offline earnings are ready) locally on your device. No push notification service, notification token or server is involved.

Advertising: The iOS version contains no advertising and no advertising SDK, and does not use Apple's advertising identifier. On Android, the App includes Google AdMob so it can offer optional rewarded video ads; these ads are currently switched off, so the App does not load or display any ads. If optional rewarded ads are switched on in the Android version, AdMob may collect device identifiers and usage data for ad personalization in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy, and you can opt out of personalized ads in your device settings.

Analytics: The App does not use any analytics SDKs on either platform. We do not track your usage patterns, location, or browsing activity, and the App does not track you across other companies' apps or websites.

Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or otherwise transfer your personal information to third parties. The only third-party services used are the platform services described above: on Android, Google Play Games Services (for leaderboards, achievements and cloud save), Google Play Billing (for purchases) and Google AdMob (for rewarded ads, currently switched off), governed by Google's privacy policy; on iOS, Apple Game Center (for leaderboards, achievements and saved games), iCloud (for saved game storage) and the App Store (for purchases), governed by Apple's privacy policy. The iOS version includes no third-party SDKs. These providers protect your data under their own privacy policies, which provide the same or equal protection of user data as stated in this policy.

Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe we have inadvertently collected such information, please contact us so we can delete it.

Data Retention

Game save data is stored locally on your device and can be deleted by uninstalling the App or using the in-app reset function. On Android, cloud save data is stored in your Google account and can be managed through Google Play Games settings. On iOS, Game Center saved games are stored in your iCloud account and can be deleted from your iCloud storage settings (in the Settings app, tap your name, then iCloud, then manage your account storage; the exact path varies by iOS version). Because we never receive your game data, we hold no copy of it to retain or delete.

Your Rights

You can delete your local game data at any time using the "Reset Game" option in Settings. On Android, you can revoke Google Play Games access through your Google account settings, and you can opt out of personalized ads in your device's ad settings. On iOS, you can sign out of Game Center in the Settings app under Game Center, and you can turn notifications off in the Settings app under Notifications. If you have a question or a request about your data, contact us at the address below.
